Engineering credibility.
With a point of view.

A portfolio for recruiters and engineering leads hiring a senior developer. The first screen establishes fit; the work establishes depth.

DESIGN STUDY 01 / 8 NOVEMBER 2026 / PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED

What the current portfolio gets right

ajaykc.com has a clear specialism: Drupal, GovCMS and accessible government platforms. Named projects, career history and direct contact details give us useful substance. The redesign keeps that professional story.

What changes

The current introduction repeats several ideas across the profile, hero and about sections. Dark panels and decorative technology imagery compete with the content. This proposal gives the introduction one clear job, moves selected work earlier, and separates personal contribution from project context.

A curated reference set

These are strong references for this brief, rather than an objective ranking of the internet’s “best” portfolios. Their principles inform an original design.

Brittany Chiang ↗

Clear engineering positioning, detailed experience and accessible navigation. Borrow the ease of finding role fit, responsibilities and a résumé. Give Ajay’s government work larger visual prominence.

Emil Kowalski ↗

A concise introduction followed by concrete projects and writing. Borrow the economy of language and sense of technical credibility. Avoid filling the homepage with generic skill claims.

Rauno Freiberg ↗

A distinctive approach to interaction and presentation. Borrow the care in typography, composition and small interactions; use it to support immediate access to the work.

Bruno Simon ↗

An interactive world demonstrates creative development skill. It is useful as a boundary reference: Ajay’s hiring audience benefits from fast scanning, so a 3D navigation experience is not the proposed direction.

Three treatments, one content strategy

01 Editorial — recommended. Warm paper, dark ink and blue accents. Architectural project artwork makes the engineering story tangible. Serif emphasis adds personality without changing the professional focus.

02 Midnight. Deep navy and sage. A more technical atmosphere and a closer evolution of the current dark site. The same project-first hierarchy keeps it readable.

03 Quiet. White, graphite and restrained sans-serif typography. The most conservative treatment for rapid evaluation. Less distinctive, but direct and calm.

The hiring journey

  1. Identify Ajay’s role, specialism and location.
  2. Inspect three selected projects and the specific contribution to each.
  3. Understand platform, frontend and delivery capabilities.
  4. Check experience and credentials.
  5. Open the résumé or start a conversation.
Before this becomes the production portfolio

Replace the labelled concept illustrations with approved project screenshots where available. Confirm current employment, availability, dates, permission to show project material, and the exact scope of each contribution. Add real technical decisions and outcomes to case studies; no invented metrics or testimonials.
