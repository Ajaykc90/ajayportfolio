# Ajay portfolio design

The project workspace is `/Users/ajaykc/Documents/Sites/Portfolio`. If moved, locate its `AGENTS.md` and `docs/PROJECT-STATE.md` before proceeding. The maintained skill source is `skills/ajay-portfolio-design/SKILL.md` in that workspace.

## Continuity

Read `docs/PROJECT-STATE.md` first. The confirmed audience is recruiters and engineering leads hiring senior developers. Ajay is a software engineer with Drupal/GovCMS and accessible public-platform experience; do not reposition him as a product designer simply to suit a visual reference.

Read the relevant project files:

- Research, comparison or art direction: `docs/RESEARCH.md` and `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.
- Biography, experience or case studies: `docs/CONTENT.md`.
- Building or verification: `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md` and `docs/QA.md`.
- Review existing visual work in `design-review/index.html` and `design-review/rationale.html` before replacing it.

## Decisions that matter

- Keep proposed treatments distinct from user-approved preferences. Editorial is the initial recommendation, not an accepted requirement. Respect subsequent steering.
- Make role fit, individual project contribution, career history and résumé access easy to scan. Let visual craft reinforce engineering credibility.
- Preserve contribution verbs and proficiency limits from the content ledger. Do not invent metrics, dates, testimonials, certifications, clearances or sole ownership.
- Treat all case-study illustrations as concept art until approved real project assets exist. Do not pass mock interfaces off as delivered client work.
- Keep the website keyboard-operable, responsive and respectful of reduced motion; verify the interaction and layout affected by a change.
- The local study and live ajaykc.com are separate. A review request does not authorise replacing the live site. The existing unpublished Sites shell is documented; do not register a duplicate without need.

## Closing a work session

Update `docs/PROJECT-STATE.md` with new user decisions, completed work and outstanding gaps. Update the single relevant source document when facts or tokens change rather than duplicating them. Record actual verification in `docs/QA.md`, with limitations. Present a concrete preview or file link when the work is visual.
